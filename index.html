ANCIENT TRADITION & PURITY

The Authentic 5-Step Vedic Bilona Workflow

Unlike commercial ghee made by extracting cream with industrial centrifuges, Kesariya Farm follows the sacred 5-step Vedic Bilona procedure described in ancient Ayurvedic texts.

STEP 1
🌿

Forest Grazing Cows

Gir cows graze freely on organic herbal pastures, producing nutrient-rich A2 milk high in beta-casein protein.

STEP 2
🏺

Clay Pot Curd Culture

Fresh A2 milk is slowly boiled in earthen pots and inoculated with natural curd starter to culture overnight.

STEP 3
🥣

Wooden Bilona Churning

Cultured curd is bi-directionally hand churned using a Shisham wooden bilona during cool morning hours to yield butter (Makkhan).

STEP 4
🔥

Slow Fire Simmering

Makkhan is gently melted over low wood fire in brass vessels until aromatic, golden, granular Vedic Ghee separates.

STEP 5
🧪

NABL Test & Glass Bottling

Ghee is lab tested for purity, free of milk solids/preservatives, and hand-poured into eco-friendly glass jars.

Why Bilona Ghee is Superior to Commercial Ghee

Commercial ghee is made in 30 minutes by separating cream with high-speed machines. Bilona Ghee takes over 25 liters of pure A2 milk to make just 1 liter of ghee, preserving all natural digestive enzymes, butyric acid, and vitamins!
